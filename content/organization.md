(TS//WB) The Office of Nonstandard Assets (ONA) is the executive agent for Special Access Program WICKER BASIN. It was formed in 1963 from the Office of Nonstandard Biological Assets (ONBA, est. 1952) by National Security Action Memorandum [[x:3]], which also established Steering Group 63 (SG-63).

## CHARTER

```doc
THE OFFICE WILL:
  ACQUIRE   locate and take custody of nonstandard assets, domestic and foreign;
  DENY      prevent their acquisition by any foreign power, by destruction where
            custody is not possible;
  EXPLOIT   evaluate them for military, intelligence and national advantage;
  CONCEAL   keep the public record free of them.

CONTAINMENT IS A METHOD. IT IS NOT A MISSION.
```

## NOTES

- **Oversight.** WICKER BASIN is a waived program. The chair and ranking member of each intelligence committee (four persons) are briefed annually. The briefing describes a biological research program. Briefing of the President is at the discretion of SG-63.
- **CUSTODIAN.** CUSTODIAN is shown on the chart outside the line of authority. It is not administered by this office, does not appear in its budget, and cannot be tasked by it. Requests are made through SG-63. Replies are not guaranteed.
- **J-4.** The staffing table authorizes 31 positions in J-4. None is filled. According to the table, none has ever been filled. J-4 holds a budget line, rooms 2-110 to 2-116 at HQ Annex 2, and a routing symbol. Correspondence addressed to J-4 is not answered.
- **Liaison.** Department of Defense (charter, funding, loaned personnel); Department of Energy laboratories (Directorate M analysis); CIA (foreign acquisition; relations are competitive); NSA (signals support); FBI (domestic cover for Quieting, without explanation); Census Bureau, Social Security Administration and IRS (J-6 Gap Analysis cells under cover of data quality review); Bureau of Prisons and military corrections (Consumables); FEMA (shelter inventory, used by J-6 to locate K-rooms).
- **Foreign.** The Soviet counterpart is known to this office as KARTOTEKA ("the card index"). Limited exchange with the United Kingdom ("the Stores") takes place through the CANNERY channel only. There is no exchange with any other government.
