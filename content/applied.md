(TS//WB) WICKER-APPLIED evaluates assets and derivative materials for military, intelligence and national use. Proposals are reviewed annually by the Applied Review Board (ARB). Sponsors are identified to the Board only. Every asset file on this server carries an APPLICATION section; a rating of U2 or above obliges the custodial directorate to submit at least one proposal every three years.

## APPLIED REVIEW BOARD — FINDINGS (EXTRACT)

```doc
WA-66-01  DISPOSAL (PA-001, K-03)
A room of 61.250 sq ft is maintained at SITE 9 (Cell 3). Its occupancy is
zero. A person placed in Cell 3 with the door closed is laminated and is not
remembered; every record that names the person is revised. No body, no
record, no witness who can say who it was.
Uses approved: Q-4 resolutions; detainees whose continued existence is a
liability; Consumables (H) at end of protocol.
FINDING: Approved 1966. Utilization is reported annually by count only.

WA-66-04  INTERROGATION BY ORDER OF ENTRY
N detainees are placed in a room of occupancy N-1 and informed of its
property. The door is closed only when the detainees have agreed among
themselves who will enter last. Information volunteered during the
agreement period has proved reliable.
FINDING: Approved, restricted to J-2 with Director's written authority.

WA-79-03  ARCHITECTURAL INSERTION
Contractors under J-2 control are placed on construction projects at
foreign official facilities. One room in each is built to a K-value.
FINDING: Phase II. Two insertions complete. Dimensions held by J-2 only.

WA-82-06  SIGNATORY NULLIFICATION
If a signatory to an agreement is laminated, the signature line is blank.
The parties remember that there was an agreement and cannot say who
signed it.
FINDING: One trial ([[x:4]]). Inconclusive. The other party re-signed.

WA-88-02  SELF-ENFORCING SHELTERS
In general war, continuity facilities will be overwhelmed by persons
seeking admission. If the rooms of those facilities are built to K-values
of suitable occupancy, the rooms will enforce their own occupancy. No guard
need fire on anyone. No record will be kept of those not admitted.
FINDING: Under review. The Board notes that it does not know where K-values
come from, and that a design which depends on them assumes they will not
change.

WA-91-02  TALLOW-E (ENDURANCE COMPOUND)
Phase II trial, 12 subjects. Marked gains in endurance and pain tolerance
were recorded in all subjects. Phase II was terminated in 1993 following
adverse behavioral effects in 9 of 12 subjects. Subjects remain under
medical observation. Program closed.

WA-95-07  DEPLOYABLE AREA-DENIAL ASSET
Field trial, [[x:8]] Range, FEB 1996. Two BA-013 specimens were released
into a 4 sq km enclosure with designated targets. The specimens ignored the
designated targets, disabled three of four tracking collars within six
hours, and were recovered after eleven days. Two handler casualties.
Specimen B was recovered dead.
FINDING: The asset cannot be directed. It can only be released.

WA-96-03  STRATEGIC EARLY WARNING
Proposes integration of the BA-009 activity index into national indications
and warning. Board concerns: (1) there is no causal model; (2) the location
of an event cannot be predicted; (3) asset activity may be affected by
observation (cf. [[EXP-97-117]]); (4) dissemination outside WICKER channels
would require disclosure of the asset.
FINDING: Deferred to FY98 review.

WA-97-01  [TITLE WITHHELD]
CHAIRMAN'S NOTE: This proposal is rejected without discussion. The Board
```
