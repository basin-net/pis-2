(TS//WB) The following directives and forms are in force. Where a summary is given, the hard copy governs.

## DIRECTIVE 64-01 — COUNTING

All work involving PA-001 is conducted under **Blind Tally**: subjects and personnel are issued numbered tokens on entry and are counted by token, never by name. All program personnel, at all sites, are counted each morning at 0800 local by J-7 (**Morning Count**). Counts are recorded as numbers only. A discrepancy is reported to J-7 even when no one is missing. *Especially* when no one is missing.

## DIRECTIVE 72-03 — RETENTION OF PUBLIC K-ROOMS

A room in public or private use that is found to be at a K-value will not be altered, closed or acquired where its retention serves program interests (observation, Gap Analysis calibration, availability for WICKER-APPLIED). J-6 will monitor. Owners and occupants will not be informed.

## DIRECTIVE 85-04 — RM 2-114

HQ Annex 2, Rm 2-114 is closed. It will not be entered with the door closed by any person for any purpose. The lock is held by J-7. The room is not to be used as overflow seating.

## DIRECTIVE 88-14 — SITE SEALING PROCEDURE (CONDITION BLACK)

Upon declaration of Condition BLACK, all access points to the affected site are to be sealed by the Site Security Element or, if it cannot be reached, by an external response team. No attempt will be made to recover personnel. The site is to be removed from all records outside WICKER channels.

## DIRECTIVE 91-02 — PUBLIC REPORTING

Public reporting of program matters is not to be suppressed. Rumor, ridicule and disbelief provide effective cover; suppression attracts attention. Personnel will not participate in, encourage, or correct public discussion. Public reports are referred to J-6 as Correlation Reports (CR-).

## DIRECTIVE 95-06 — PERSONNEL SCREENING

Personnel in PA-001 assignments will undergo psychological screening every 30 days. Personnel are not to compare unexplained grief, habits they cannot account for, or other symptoms with colleagues. Report them to the Site Medical Officer.

## SOP 4-1 (REV. F) — CONTAINMENT BREACH RESPONSE

Supersedes Rev. E, effective 06 OCT 97. Hard copy only. One copy is held at every duty station.

## FORM W-3 — REQUEST FOR CUSTODIAL RECORDS

Submit to Custodial Records, Rm B-114. Need-to-know must be certified by your division chief. Older stock printed "ONBA FORM W-3 (REV. 9-61)" remains valid.
