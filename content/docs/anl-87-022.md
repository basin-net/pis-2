SUBJECT:  LAMINA ANALYSIS, K-01 (ROOM B-2, HELD IN SITU)
DATE:     22 JUN 1987

1. A core was taken through the floor of Room B-2. Room B-2 has been held in situ under lease since 1963 and has not been entered with the door closed since 27 OCT 1962. The core shows a single lamina.

2. DNA was extracted from the lamina. The lamina is from one female individual. Age at lamination, from bone mineral: 10 to 13 years.

3. A reference sample from R. D. PRUITT (shelter warden, INC-62-001) was obtained in 1987 under medical pretext (Q-1).

4. RESULT: The lamina individual is a child of R. D. PRUITT. Probability of paternity greater than 0.999.

5. R. D. PRUITT has no children. County birth records, school enrollment 1956-62 and the 1960 census record no child of PRUITT. The 1960 census enumeration sheet for the PRUITT household lists PRUITT, R. D. (line 14) and PRUITT, M. (line 15). Line 16 is blank. The next household begins at line 17.

6. NOTE: Attachment B to INC-62-001 (routing slip, 1963) gives the girl's first name. Its text has not been revised. No other document bearing the name of a laminated subject is known to have survived.

7. RECOMMENDATION: No further contact with PRUITT.
