ANALYSIS ANL-79-014                 TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN
DIRECTORATE P, WITH J-1 (MEDICAL)
SUBJECT:  CELL 3 FLOOR LEVEL. COMPRESSION OF LAMINAE.
DATE:     14 SEP 1979

1. SITE 9 Cell 3 is K-03: 7 ft 0 in by 8 ft 9 in (61.250 sq ft), ceiling 8 ft 0 in, occupancy 0 ([[applied|WA-66-01]]). By [[PA-001]] (section 3) one subject raises a floor of this area by approx. 12 mm (1/2 in.). Utilization from 1966 to 31 AUG 1979 is 117 by count. The floor should stand approx. 1.44 m (4 ft 9 in) above the original slab.

2. It stands 2-15/16 in. (75 mm).

3. J-3 has measured the floor after every use since the first, in 1966, at the opening of the door and again at 30 hours. Until the sixth subject the floor rose by about 1/2 in. each time. Since then it has stood, at every measurement, between 2-7/8 and 2-15/16 in. It has never been measured higher. Cell 3 is acoustically isolated and its intercom is off while the door is closed, so nothing is known of how the compression sounds. Operators have reported nothing.

4. Two cores were taken through the floor on 20 AUG with the door held open. Each shows a surface course of 1 mm and beneath it 117 laminae. The count agrees with the utilization count exactly. The uppermost lamina is at full thickness (12 mm). Below it each lamina is thinner than the one above. The lowest are less than 0.1 mm, thinner than paper. Under the microscope even these are complete sheets of broken bone, soft tissue, hair and dried fluid, sealed above and below. No lamina has merged with another. Nothing is missing from them. They have been compressed.

5. The value. The floor of Cell 3 does not rise above about 3 in. That is 1/32 of the height of its ceiling.

6. J-1 study PSY-77-004 raised the floors of six mock offices at SITE 3 by stages, without notice, under staff who worked in them daily. The staff were asked weekly whether anything about the room had changed. The median height at which a person first reported that the room was "lower", "smaller" or "wrong" was 1/32 of the ceiling height. The study was made for another purpose (J-6 Reconciliation of altered premises). It was completed before this analysis began.

7. The two figures agree to within 1/16 in.

8. The door is not taken into account. A step of 1/4 in. at a sill is felt by anyone who crosses it; a step of 3 in. stops a cart. The compression value concerns the room as it is seen and felt by those inside it. In every K-room whose floor has been noticed, it was noticed at the door.

9. FINDING. When a lamination would raise the floor of a K-room past 1/32 of its ceiling height, the laminae already there are compressed, silently and without loss, so that the floor stays below it. The new lamina is laid at full thickness on top.

10. RECOMMENDATION. Cell 3 need not be excavated. Its cores give an exact count and may be used by J-7 to audit WA-66-01 utilization.
