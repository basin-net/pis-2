ANALYSIS ANL-80-031                 TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN
J-2 ANALYSIS DIVISION
SUBJECT:  PA-001 (HEADCOUNT): ORIGIN OF K-VALUES. COMPETING HYPOTHESES.
DATE:     14 APR 1980

1. The question is why these floor areas and not others, and why these occupancies. Four hypotheses are considered against the 29 values then known.

2. H1 -- SHELTER

   For:
   a. For 24 of 29 values the occupancy is close to one person per 10 sq ft. Ten square feet per person is the planning figure of the federal public fallout shelter program from 1961 onward.
   b. Every value is a whole number of square inches, as an area computed from drawings dimensioned in feet and inches will be.
   c. The first recorded event (K-01, INC-62-001) took place in a designated public fallout shelter during the Cuban crisis. The posted capacity of that shelter was 14. The K-01 occupancy is 14.
   d. Incidence is concentrated where buildings are dimensioned in feet and inches. Soviet incidence is about 1/40 of U.S. incidence.

   Against:
   e. The low values. K-03 (0 persons in 61 sq ft). K-07 (2 in 418). K-04 (3 in 140). K-12 (6 in 198). A shelter survey does not assign two persons to 418 sq ft or no one to 61.

   Unless:
   f. The low values are rooms with assigned functions -- a records room that no one is to enter, quarters for two senior persons, a communications room for a crew of three, an infirmary of six beds. That would mean the values are not drawn from a general standard but from a plan for particular rooms in particular facilities.

   Assessment: H1 explains the regular values. With (f) it explains the low values. It requires that the phenomenon follow a specific plan.

3. H2 -- SOVIET DEVICE
   For: timing (October 1962).
   Against: Soviet incidence is lower, not higher. KARTOTEKA material passed through CANNERY (1977) shows the Soviets first recorded the effect, which they call UPLOTNENIE, in 1964 and were surprised by it.
   Assessment: Unlikely.

4. H3 -- SHIELDING MASS
   The laminated person adds mass to the floor. In a shelter, added mass means added protection for those inside. On this view the effect "turns the excess into shielding".
   Against: 5 mm of tissue changes the protection factor of a floor by less than one percent. Nor does shielding require that records and memory be revised.
   Assessment: Attractive. Does not explain the record effect.

5. H4 -- CENSUS
   Something is counting the population and setting limits on it.
   Against: No effect at any scale larger than a room has been observed.
   Assessment: No evidence.

6. CONCLUSION
H1 is the only hypothesis consistent with every known value. If H1 is correct, there exists, or existed, a schedule of rooms and occupancies, prepared in or before October 1962, and the K-values are its entries. The record effect may also come from the schedule.

7. RECOMMENDATION
A search of 1961-62 civil defense and continuity-of-government planning records, beginning with material held by this office from SITE 1.
