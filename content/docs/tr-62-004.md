```transcript
ONBA CASE 62-K-1                                  INTERVIEW, REEL 2 OF 2
DATE:        09 NOV 1962
PLACE:       RESIDENCE OF SUBJECT, [[x:12]]. LIVING ROOM. RECORDER ON
             COFFEE TABLE. MANTEL CLOCK AUDIBLE THROUGHOUT.
SUBJECT:     R. D. PRUITT (SHELTER WARDEN, COURTHOUSE B-2)
PRESENT:     MRS. M. PRUITT (BRIEFLY)
INTERVIEWER: [[x:9]] (ONBA)

         [RECORDER STARTED. FOOTSTEPS. TWO CUPS SET DOWN.]
MRS. P:  There you are.
INT:     Thank you, ma'am.
         [FOOTSTEPS. A DOOR CLOSES.]
INT:     [COUGHS] Excuse me.
         [PAPERS]
INT:     Mr. Pruitt, how many people did you admit to the shelter on the
         night of the twenty-seventh?
PRUITT:  Fourteen.
INT:     Your log says fifteen.
PRUITT:  [EXHALES] I know what it says. I wrote it. I don't -- I don't
         know why I wrote it.
INT:     The sign on the door says fourteen.
PRUITT:  That's right. Fourteen. That's the rule.
INT:     Who closed the door?
PRUITT:  I did. I closed the door. I always close the door. That's the
         warden's job. You count them in, and you, uh -- you close the
         door.
INT:     Was anyone outside when you closed it?
PRUITT:  No. Nobody was outside.
         [PAUSE, 6 SEC. HIS CHAIR CREAKS.]
PRUITT:  [QUIETLY] Everybody was inside.
INT:     Mr. Pruitt, do you have any children?
PRUITT:  No.
         [PAUSE]
PRUITT:  No, sir. My wife and I, we -- [COUGHS] -- no.
INT:     There's a girl's bicycle in your garage.
         [PAUSE, 14 SEC. CLOCK. CHAIR CREAKS. SUBJECT SNIFFS.]
PRUITT:  I'd like you to go now.
INT:     Mr. Pruitt --
PRUITT:  I'd like you to go now.
         [A CHAIR IS PUSHED BACK. RECORDER STOPPED.]

[END OF REEL 2]
```
