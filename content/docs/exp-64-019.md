POST-TEST
- No observer can state why ten tokens were issued.
- The J-1 requisition reads "TEN (10)". No observer recalls the requisition.
- Film reel 2 shows ten subjects entering. No one has identified subject 10.
- Floor of Cell 2 raised approx. 5 mm. Surface soft and warm at 1404, hard by the next morning, with the finish of the original sealed concrete.
- Cell 2 was isolated after EXP-63-001 (SITE 1, open cell), when all present heard the process, and four requested transfer. J-1 recommended that no operator again listen to a lamination. The operators of 17 FEB therefore report nothing.

FINDING
The phenomenon removes names. It does not remove numbers. A count made without names survives.

RECOMMENDATION
Blind Tally for all PA-001 work. A daily anonymous count of all program personnel.
Adopted as Directive 64-01, 02 MAR 1964.

NOTE (J-7, 04 MAR 1964)
The first count of SITE 9 personnel under Directive 64-01, taken 03 MAR, found 41. The SITE 9 staffing table authorizes 42 positions. No vacancy is recorded. The control room has four chairs and five coffee cups.
