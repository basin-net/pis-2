ANALYSIS ANL-86-011                 TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN
DIRECTORATE P
SUBJECT:  K-31 AND THE SURVEYOR RECORD
DATE:     03 FEB 1986

1. K-31 (325.333 sq ft, occupancy 0) was first recorded 19 MAR 1985 ([[INC-85-007]]).

2. 325.333 sq ft lies inside the ranges swept by SURVEYOR Pass A (40-450) and Pass B (40-700). The traversal log ([[SUR-73-F]], annex) records the Accordion at exactly 46,848 sq in (325.333 sq ft) with the door closed and subjects inside:

      11 AUG 1965   N = 4    NO EVENT
      14 JUN 1968   N = 12   NO EVENT

   A value of occupancy 0 would have laminated every subject both times.

3. Rm 2-114 was used for closed-door meetings 2,214 times between 1971 and 19 MAR 1985 without incident.

4. Either the SURVEYOR log and the HQ Annex 2 booking log are both wrong, in the same place, or K-31 did not exist before 19 MAR 1985.

5. If K-31 did not exist before 19 MAR 1985, K-values are not fixed. Something can add one. Something added one, of occupancy 0, at the dimensions of a room in this building, in time for a meeting held in it.

6. RECOMMENDATION
Determine who or what can add a K-value.

[HANDWRITTEN, MARGIN:] File. Do not pursue. -- DIR
