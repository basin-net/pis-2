::: audio aud-89-001.wav | AUD-89-001 | HQ WATCH LOGGING RECORDER, CH. 3, 14 MAR 1989 0316-0318

```transcript
LAST RECORDED VOICE TRANSMISSION, SITE 22 TO HQ WATCH
14 MAR 1989, 0317 LOCAL

SITE 22:   ...all levels accounted for except Level Three. Level Three
           is not responding. The Level Three door is open. It was
           opened from the inside. Repeat, from the inside. We are
           sending two down now. Will call back in ten min--
HQ WATCH:  Site Twenty-Two, say again your last.
HQ WATCH:  Site Twenty-Two, HQ Watch. Over.
           [NO FURTHER TRANSMISSION]
```

::: audio aud-89-002.wav | AUD-89-002 | SAME RECORDER, CH. 3, 14 MAR 1989 0921 (EXCERPT, FIRST 0:30 OF 6:02)

```doc
NOTE: Between 0317 and 0920 local, the HQ Watch log records fourteen attempts to reestablish contact. At 0921 the line from SITE 22 opened for six minutes and two seconds. No voice was recorded. Condition BLACK was declared 17 MAR 1989 and Directive 88-14 was executed.

The line from SITE 22 has opened on four occasions since 1989, most recently 02 OCT 1997. None has been acknowledged. See Directive 88-14, para. 6.
```
