EXPERIMENT REPORT                   TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN

EXP. NO. ........... EXP-97-117
ASSET .............. BA-009 (LANTERN)
LOCATION ........... SITE 3, LEVEL 4, CELL 4-C (FROM CONTROL ROOM 4-2)
PRINCIPAL .......... DR. [[x:10]] (BIOACOUSTICS)
PURPOSE ............ Determine whether low-frequency acoustic stimulus produces a measurable response in BA-009 during the dormant phase.

T+00 SEC   Stimulus begins. 8 Hz, 70 dB.
T+12 SEC   No response.
T+21 SEC   Stimulus increased to 14 Hz. Asset orientation changes toward
           the speaker array.
T+29 SEC   Ocular retroreflection observed WITHOUT illumination. Cell
           lighting confirmed off.
T+34 SEC   Control room personnel report pressure in the ears.
           Monitor 2 fails. Reel recorder fails.
T+40 SEC   Experiment halted by order of the Site Director.
T+41 SEC   Asset returns to dormant posture.

POST-TEST: On the night of 30 JUL, both control room operators reported the same dream. The content of the dream is recorded separately ([[denied:PSY-97-022|PSY-97-022]], restricted).

RECOMMENDATION: No further acoustic stimulus of BA-009.
APPROVED: [[x:12]], SITE DIRECTOR, SITE 3.
