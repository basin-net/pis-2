ANALYSIS ANL-85-016                 TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN
DIRECTORATE P, WITH J-7 (ESCORT) AND [[x:10]] LABORATORY (DOE)
SUBJECT:  LAMINA ANALYSIS, K-31 (RM 2-114, HQ ANNEX 2)
DATE:     29 MAR 1985

1. Five cores, 50 mm in diameter, were taken through the floor of Rm 2-114 on 25 MAR: at the door, at the head and the foot of the conference table, and in the two far corners. The door was wedged open and held by a J-7 officer throughout. The team worked in shifts of 40 minutes. The floor stands 71 mm above the corridor. Every core shows, from the top: a surface course approx. 6 mm thick; 31 laminae, together approx. 65 mm; the original slab. The core holes were filled with epoxy grout. The ceiling is 8 ft 0 in. The floor is 3/16 in. below the compression value for that ceiling ([[ANL-79-014]]) and has not been compressed.

2. The surface course is carpet: pile, primary and secondary backing and underlay, in their original order, bonded together and rigid. It contains no tissue. The pile shows none of the wear that fourteen years of traffic at the door would have left. It is the only part of the floor that is floor material. Its lower face merges into the upper face of the uppermost lamina over about 1 mm. This is the only boundary in the core that is not sharp.

3. Each lamina is a sheet of nearly uniform thickness (1.3 to 3.0 mm, mean 2.1 mm) that runs without a break across the whole floor. The laminae lie flat on one another like the pages of a closed book. Each is made of four things. There is no carpet and no cement in any of them.
   a. Bone, finely broken: fragments from 0.1 to 4 mm, the coarsest at the door and the finest at the far wall, with their long axes in the plane of the sheet and radiating from the door.
   b. Soft tissue, flattened to a dark brown leathery film 0.2 to 0.5 mm thick, which binds the fragments. Skin, muscle and fat can be told apart under the microscope.
   c. Hair, head and body, lying in the plane of the sheet parallel to the fragments, with the roots attached.
   d. Dried body fluid (blood, serum, fat), which fills the spaces between the fragments and stains the tissue. A film of it less than 0.1 mm thick seals the upper and the lower face of every lamina.

4. Nothing crosses a boundary. In five cores and 31 laminae no fragment, strand or fibre has been found in a neighbouring lamina. The laminae are not fused to one another or to the slab. A lamina can be lifted from its neighbour along its film. We do not know what keeps them apart. We added nothing.

5. All laminae are dry and firm. None shows decomposition, odour, or microbial or insect activity, six days after the event. Soft tissue of this age should be neither dry nor sound.

6. COUNT. There are 31 laminae in each of the five cores. Thirty-one sets of clothing were bagged from the room ([[FWO-85-0412]]). Line 32 of the sign-in sheet records an OBSERVER ([[INC-85-007]], Attachment A). There is no 32nd lamina.

7. THE 31, FROM THE LAMINAE.
   Sex (pelvis, skull): 22 male, 9 female.
   Age (sutures, pubic symphysis): 24 to 61 years; 9 with grey hair.
   Blood group (dried blood): O 14, A 12, B 4, AB 1.
   Teeth: amalgam in 24, a gold crown in 3, a full upper denture in lamina 9.
   Lamina 17 holds a flattened cardiac pacemaker. Its serial number can be read. J-6 enquired of the manufacturer under cover of a recall audit. The manufacturer's implant registry has no entry for this serial. The serials on either side of it are registered.
   J-1 holds no personnel or medical record that corresponds to any of the 31.

8. ON THE PROCESS. The findings at K-01 ([[ANL-87-022]]) are found here 31 times. Each subject was taken into the floor separately and the force came from the door in every case. The clothing was found laid out as if the subjects had been seated at the table and along the walls ([[INC-85-007]], para. 1). That arrangement has no counterpart in the cores.

9. Only the surface is floor. The 31 are what they were, in sheets, under 6 mm of carpet. FWO-85-0412 reports the floor "hard as the slab". That holds for the top 6 mm only.

10. RECOMMENDATION: The cores are to be held in the lamina library at SITE 3. The surface course of Rm 2-114 is not to be removed.
