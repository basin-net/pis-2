Q-1 INTERVIEW TR-71-117                  FE-71-02, EVENT 1 (22 MAY 1971)
DATE:        30 JUN 1971
PLACE:       RESIDENCE OF SUBJECT
SUBJECT:     [[x:12]] (FEMALE, 34). ATTENDED EVENT 1.
INTERVIEWER: J-6 FIELD OFFICER (COVER: COUNTY SURVEY TAKER)

INT:     How many people live in this house?
SUBJ:    Three. Me and the girls.
INT:     There are four chairs at the table.
SUBJ:    It's a set. They come in fours.
INT:     There are four toothbrushes in the bathroom.
SUBJ:    [11 SEC.]
         I keep a spare.
INT:     The fourth one has been used.
SUBJ:    I would like you to leave now.
         [A CHILD ENTERS.]
CHILD:   Is he asking about the tall one?
SUBJ:    Go back to your room, honey.
CHILD:   The tall one is flat now.
SUBJ:    [INAUDIBLE]
         Please leave.

[END OF RECORDING]

INTERVIEWER'S NOTES
Daughter, about 6. Elder daughter, about 9, did not appear. Men's work boots, size 11, by the back door. Subject wears a wedding ring. When asked about it she said she has never been married, then took it off and put it back on. Q-1 status maintained. No further action.
