FACILITIES WORK ORDER                               FWO-85-0412
BUILDING:   HQ ANNEX 2
ROOM:       2-114 (CONFERENCE -- ASSIGNED J-4)
REQUESTED:  19 MAR 1985, 1420    BY: J-7 (INC-85-007)
ROOM DIMENSIONS (AS BUILT 1971; VERIFIED 20 MAR 85): 20'-4" x 16'-0"

WORK REQUIRED
1. Floor surface raised approx. 2-13/16 in. above corridor floor,
   uniformly. Cause: ______________. Do NOT core, sand or remove.
2. Door binding on floor. Remove door, trim bottom 3 in., rehang.
3. Install ramp threshold, both sides.
4. Remove and bag 31 sets of clothing and personal effects.
   Deliver to J-7, Rm 1-020. Do not sort.
5. Install keyed lock (J-7 key only). Post sign: CLOSED. DO NOT USE.

COMPLETED:  22 MAR 1985

TECHNICIAN'S REMARKS
Floor is warm to the hand. Carpet is stuck down into it, left in place.
Room smells of nothing at all. Fourteen cups washed and returned to the
J-4 kitchen. There are 31 hooks on the J-4 coat rack and nothing on
any of them.
