1. CIRCUMSTANCES
On the evening of 27 OCT 1962 the county civil defense director ordered a precautionary occupation of designated shelters. The courthouse basement shelter (Room B-2) was opened by the shelter warden, Mr. R. D. PRUITT, at 2140. Persons arriving were logged and admitted. The warden closed and secured the shelter door at 2214.

2. EVENT
Occupants state that "something happened on the floor" immediately after the door was secured. None can describe it. Several became distressed. The warden reopened the door at approx. 2219. No occupant was injured. No one was missing.

3. FINDINGS (31 OCT 1962)
a. A set of clothing (girl's coat, dress, shoes; size approx. 10-12 years) lay on the floor just inside the door. No occupant claimed it.
b. The floor of Room B-2 is covered by a continuous, translucent, slightly elastic layer approx. 5 mm thick. Samples are human tissue (skin, muscle, bone mineral) in uniform suspension. The layer is bonded to the concrete.
c. Room B-2 measures 14 ft 6 in by 10 ft 2 in (147.417 sq ft). The posted shelter capacity is 14.
d. Shelter log: see Attachment A.

4. ASSESSMENT
Unknown. ONBA requests custody of Room B-2.
