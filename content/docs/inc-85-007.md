```doc
INCIDENT REPORT INC-85-007          TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN
DATE:      19 MAR 1985. DISCOVERED 1310L.
LOCATION:  HQ ANNEX 2, CONFERENCE ROOM 2-114
ASSET:     PA-001

1. At 1310 a facilities technician answering a report that the door of Rm 2-114 was "sticking" found the room unoccupied and the door closed. On the floor were 31 sets of clothing and personal effects, placed as if the wearers had been seated at the table and in chairs along the walls. Fourteen coffee cups on the table. The room was warm, and so were several of the cups.

2. The floor of Rm 2-114 is raised uniformly by approx. 2-13/16 in. The carpet over the floor surface was warm and yielding underfoot (36 C at 1330) and was rigid by 1900 the same day. Directorate P confirms lamination. Personnel in Rm 2-214, directly above, heard a loud cracking and grinding at approx. 0905, lasting about 20 seconds, the longest on record. It was attributed to work in the adjoining building. None filed a report. One telephoned J-3 at 1100 to ask whether maintenance was working on the floor.

3. The room measures 20 ft 4 in by 16 ft 0 in (325.333 sq ft). This is not a known K-value. It is designated K-31. Occupancy is assessed as 0. See para. 6.

4. No personnel are missing. See Morning Count, 20 MAR ([[MC-85-03]]).

5. The room booking log shows Rm 2-114 booked for 19 MAR, 0900-1100. The booking name is blank. The log shows Rm 2-114 used for closed-door meetings 2,214 times between the opening of HQ Annex 2 (1971) and 19 MAR 1985, without incident.

6. A sign-in sheet was found on the table (Attachment A). Thirty-one lines are blank. Line 32 records an OBSERVER. Inquiry sent to CUSTODIAN through SG-63, 21 MAR. No reply.

7. J-1 Medical reports 47 consultations for "unexplained grief" by HQ Annex 2 personnel in the week beginning 19 MAR.

8. Rm 2-114 closed ([[directives|Directive 85-04]]). Work order [[FWO-85-0412]]. Floor cored 25 MAR, door held open, J-7 escort ([[ANL-85-016]]).
```
