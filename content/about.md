**PIS-2 // WICKER BASIN is a work of fiction.** The Office of Nonstandard Assets, Special Access Program WICKER BASIN, its sites, documents, recordings and every person named in them are invented. Real historical events (such as the Cuban Missile Crisis of October 1962) appear only as background. Nothing here describes a real program, and no real person is depicted.

It is a collaborative horror setting in the tradition of the SCP Foundation and the Backrooms, written as the internal web server of a 1990s classified program. Unlike those settings, the organization here is not trying to protect anyone. It acquires, denies, exploits and conceals.

The site is built from plain text files in a Git repository. Anyone can propose a new document. See `README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository.
