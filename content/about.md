**PIS-2 // WICKER BASIN is a work of fiction.** The Office of Nonstandard Assets, Special Access Program WICKER BASIN, its sites, documents, recordings and every person named in them are invented. Real historical events (such as the Cuban Missile Crisis of October 1962) appear only as background. Nothing here describes a real program, and no real person is depicted.

It is a collaborative horror setting in the tradition of the SCP Foundation and the Backrooms, written as the internal web server of a 1990s classified program. Unlike those settings, the organization here is not trying to protect anyone. It acquires, denies, exploits and conceals.

The site is built from plain text files in a Git repository. Anyone can propose a new document. See `README.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository.

**Sound.** Voices in the recordings are computer speech synthesis. Room sounds, coughs and sighs are built from recordings on Wikimedia Commons, almost all in the public domain, then cut up and processed. "Drag chair" and "Drag chair 3" are by Gravity Sound, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Full sources: `tools/audio/samples/CREDITS.md` in the repository.

**PIS-2 // WICKER BASIN はフィクションです。** 登場する機関、計画、施設、文書、録音、人物はすべて架空です。キューバ危機などの実在の歴史的出来事は背景としてのみ扱っており、実在の計画や人物を描いたものではありません。

SCP財団や Backrooms の流れをくむ共同創作ホラーで、1990年代の機密計画の内部サーバーという体裁で書かれています。投稿方法はリポジトリの `README.md` と `CONTRIBUTING.md` を参照してください。

**音声について。** 録音の声はコンピューターの音声合成です。物音、咳、ため息は Wikimedia Commons の録音（ほとんどがパブリックドメイン）を切り出して加工したものです。「Drag chair」「Drag chair 3」は Gravity Sound の作品で、CC BY 4.0（https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/）で提供されています。出典の一覧はリポジトリの `tools/audio/samples/CREDITS.md` にあります。
