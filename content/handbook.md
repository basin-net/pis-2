(U) This handbook is issued to all personnel on read-in. It governs the form of every document on this server.

## 1. WHAT IS AN ASSET

A nonstandard thing is registered as an **asset** when it can be held or the effect can be reproduced. A one-time event remains a **case** (CR- or INC-) until it recurs. "Nonstandard" is the official term; "anomalous", "paranormal" and similar words are not used.

## 3. RATINGS

Every asset carries three ratings, written CLASS / UTILITY / DISCLOSURE (for example, **IV / U4 / D0**).

| CLASS | CONTAINMENT |
|---|---|
| {ctr} I | Inert or fully compliant. Standard handling. |
| {ctr} II | Physically dangerous. Hazard manageable by conventional means. |
| {ctr} III | Dangerous. Behavior not fully explained. Two-person rule. |
| {ctr} IV | Effects extend beyond physical contact. Exposure limits apply to observers. |
| {ctr} V | Containment depends on procedures whose rationale is not available to the custodian. |

| UTILITY | NATIONAL VALUE |
|---|---|
| {ctr} U0 | Liability. No foreseeable use. |
| {ctr} U1 | Research value only. |
| {ctr} U2 | Derivative materials or methods may have use. |
| {ctr} U3 | Operational use demonstrated or likely. |
| {ctr} U4 | Strategic. |

| DISCLOSURE | RISK OF PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE |
|---|---|
| {ctr} D0 | Self-concealing. The asset removes the evidence of itself. |
| {ctr} D1 | Low. |
| {ctr} D2 | Moderate. Folklore exists. |
| {ctr} D3 | Exposure likely without action by J-6. |

## 4. STATUS CODES

CONTAINED · HELD IN SITU · MONITORED · UNCONFIRMED · LOST · SEALED · **DENIED** (destroyed to prevent foreign acquisition) · **RELEASED** (deliberately set loose) · **EXPENDED** (used up in operations or protocol)

## 5. DOCUMENT PREFIXES

INC- incident · EXP- experiment · SUR- survey program · FE- field evaluation · WA- WICKER-APPLIED proposal · ANL- analysis · TR- transcript · AUD- audio · PSY- psychological · NX- necropsy · MC- Morning Count · FWO- facilities work order · Q- Quieting case · K- HEADCOUNT value

## 6. ACCESS

WICKER-1 (aware of program) · WICKER-2 (support) · **WICKER-3** (analyst, custodial staff) · WICKER-4 (Site Director, Principal Investigator) · WICKER-5 (directorate head and above).

{small} Note (J-7): CUSTODIAN designators do not correspond to any clearance level administered by J-7. "CUSTODIAN-7" is not a clearance level. Requests for CUSTODIAN read-in will not be processed.

## 7. GLOSSARY

| TERM | MEANING |
|---|---|
| Consumable (H) / (A) | Test subject, human or animal. Requisitioned through J-1. |
| Volunteer | A Consumable (H) who has signed a consent form. The form does not describe the protocol. |
| Expended | Used up. Applies to assets, materials and Consumables. |
| Correlation Report | A public report (rumor, letter, call-in program) that may concern an asset. Referred to J-6. |
| Residual | Physical or documentary trace left after an event. |
| Reconciliation | Revision of public records after an event so that they agree with each other. |
| Gap Analysis | Search of public records for numbering gaps, blank lines and unexplained quantities. Principal means of detecting PA-001 events in the public. |
| Quieting | Management of witnesses. Q-1 observe. Q-2 discredit. Q-3 relocate. Q-4 resolve. |
| K-value / K-room | A floor area at which PA-001 applies; a room at that area. A K-room is "live" while it is at value. |
| Lamination | What happens to a person in excess of a K-room's occupancy: forced into the floor in 6 to 9 seconds, with a sound of fracture and grinding. The floor hardens over them to its original character. |
| Shortfall | What the people who knew that person feel afterwards. |
| Blind Tally | Counting by numbered token, without names. |
| Morning Count | Daily count of all personnel by number. |
| Count Day | 27 October. All personnel are counted in person. |
| Sealing | Permanent closure of a site under Directive 88-14. |
