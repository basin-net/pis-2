1. (TS//WB) DESCRIPTION
Biped, approx. 1.2 m, capable of rapid quadrupedal movement. Grey-green integument. A row of erectile spines runs from the crown to the base of the spine and is raised when the animal is disturbed. Large, dark, forward-set eyes. Feeds by puncture wound to the neck or thorax of the prey and extraction of blood. Four specimens were recovered. Specimen B died in 1996 (necropsy NX-96-002). Specimen count corrected this revision.

2. (TS//WB) CONTAINMENT
Individual cells, Level 2. Specimens will not be housed within line of sight of one another (see INC-96-014). Feeding: live livestock, twice weekly, through the remote feeding port. Handlers will vary the feeding schedule without pattern.

3. (TS//WB) NOTES
Specimens learn handler routines quickly. Specimen C has been observed waiting at the feeding port before the scheduled feed, including on occasions when the schedule was changed without notice to Level 2 staff.

EXP-97-119 recorded simultaneous, coordinated action by specimens A and D in separate cells, with no line of sight and no known means of communication.

4. (TS//WB) APPLICATION
U1. Evaluated as a deployable area-denial asset under [[applied|WA-95-07]]. Terminated 1996. Board finding: "The asset cannot be directed. It can only be released."
