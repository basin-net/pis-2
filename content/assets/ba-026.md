The asset is to be kept in the acoustically isolated chamber at Level 6 at all times. No audio monitoring equipment of any kind is to be connected to the chamber or installed within Level 6. 3. (TS//WB) PERSONNEL
Personnel assigned to Level 6 are rotated every 30 days and are not to be reassigned to Level 6 within 12 months. Personnel who report hearing singing outside Level 6 are to complete Form W-12 and report to the Site Medical Officer. They are not to discuss the report with other personnel.
