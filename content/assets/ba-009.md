1. (TS//WB) DESCRIPTION
Winged bipedal organism. Standing height 2.0 m; wingspan estimated 3.1 m (full extension has never been measured). No head distinct from the thorax. Paired ocular structures at the upper thorax produce strong retroreflection in the red spectrum under illumination. No vocalization has been recorded in custody since 1971. The asset has not been observed to feed.

2. (TS//WB) CONTAINMENT
Cell 4-C is maintained unlit. Illumination of the cell is prohibited except during scheduled observation, maximum 90 seconds. Observers will not maintain direct eye contact with the asset for longer than 10 seconds. A minimum of two (2) personnel will be present during observation. No person is to enter Cell 4-C for any reason.

3. (TS//WB) BEHAVIOR
The asset is inactive for more than 99 percent of observed time. Periods of activity (wing extension; orientation toward the north wall of the cell) have been recorded on 41 occasions since acquisition. J-2 analysis (ANL-88-004) found that 37 of these 41 periods preceded a mass-casualty event elsewhere in the world by 2 to 19 days. No causal mechanism has been identified. The direction of orientation does not reliably indicate the location of the subsequent event.

5. (TS//WB) STANDING ORDER 9-A
When the activity index exceeds 7.0, the Duty Officer will notify the J-2 Watch (ext. 4410) within 15 minutes. Do not attempt to predict the location of a subsequent event. Do not inform personnel outside WICKER channels.
