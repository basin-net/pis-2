MEMORANDUM                          TOP SECRET // WICKER BASIN // NOFORN

TO:       DIRECTOR, ONA
FROM:     J-4
DATE:     15 MAR 1985
SUBJECT:  BOX 61. WHERE THE K-VALUES COME FROM.

1. During disposal review of the SITE 1 transfer boxes (J4-INV-84-118), J-4 found in Box 61 a single carbon page with the following heading:

        ANNEX K TO EMERGENCY PLAN D-62
        EMERGENCY OCCUPANCY SCHEDULE
        PART I -- SPACES (CONTINUED)                  PAGE 7 OF 9

2. The page lists rooms in a facility identified only as FACILITY 04, with the floor area and authorized occupancy of each. Items 171 to 178 are legible and are transcribed below. Items 163-170 and 179-189 are too faint to read on the carbon. The right-hand column is ours.

  ITEM  ROOM                         FLOOR AREA    AUTH. OCC.   ONA
  171   B-12  RECORDS                 61.250 SF        0        K-03
  172   B-14  DORMITORY 2            311.438 SF       31        K-08
  173   B-15  PRINCIPALS' QUARTERS   418.000 SF        2        K-07
  174   B-16  DORMITORY 3            529.375 SF       52        --
  175   B-17  WASH                   133.333 SF       13        --
  176   B-18  COMMUNICATIONS         140.000 SF        3        K-04
  177   B-19  MESS                   552.000 SF       55        --
  178   B-20  INFIRMARY              198.000 SF        6        K-12

3. Every page of the schedule carries the same footnote and certification. On page 7 they read:

        NOTE: PERSONS IN EXCESS OF AUTHORIZED OCCUPANCY SHALL NOT BE
        ADMITTED. NO RECORD SHALL BE KEPT OF PERSONS NOT ADMITTED.

        CERTIFIED 27 OCT 1962 1652 EDT          [INITIALS ILLEGIBLE]
        PART II -- AUTHORIZED PRINCIPALS -- SEE PAGE 9

4. J-4 concludes:

   a. K-values are not natural constants. They are the floor areas of the rooms in this schedule, and K-occupancy is the occupancy the schedule authorizes. Five of the eight legible items match known K-values to the square inch. Items 174, 175 and 177 are values this office has not found. SURVEYOR could not have found them: their occupancies are above the number of subjects it used.

   b. The footnote describes PA-001 exactly. "Shall not be admitted": the persons in excess do not remain occupants. "No record shall be kept": the persons in excess are not recorded, and are not remembered.

   c. The schedule was certified at 1652 on 27 OCT 1962. The first recorded event (K-01) was at 2214 the same day.

   d. Part II lists "Authorized Principals". If these are persons, they stand outside the schedule. They may not be counted against any occupancy, and the record provision may not apply to them.

   e. The designator CUSTODIAN first appears in SG-63 correspondence in November 1962 (Box 68). SG-63 Instruction 63-2 requires that all matters concerning 1962 civil defense records be notified to CUSTODIAN.

   f. If the schedule can be read, it may be able to be written.

5. Page 9 was not in Box 61. The original of the schedule has not been located.

6. RECOMMENDATIONS
   a. That all J-4 personnel be briefed 19 MAR, 0900, Rm 2-114.
   b. That the Director be briefed 20 MAR.
   c. That the Director inform the SG-63 principals and the chairs of the oversight committees directly, and not through CUSTODIAN.

ENCL: Carbon, Annex K, page 7 (1 sheet).



                                 [SIGNATURE BLOCK BLANK --
                                  PEN IMPRESSION PRESENT]
